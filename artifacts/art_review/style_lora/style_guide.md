Our house style is a form of painterly digital realism that emulates traditional oil painting. The defining characteristic is dramatic, high-contrast lighting that carves figures out of moody, atmospheric environments. Compositions are built on a strong value structure to ensure immediate readability and a clear focal point, even at a small scale. The overall mood is epic and dramatic, capturing a sense of classic fantasy illustration that feels both grounded and magical.

### Medium & finish
Aim for a digital painting that feels like a traditional oil or acrylic piece on canvas. A subtle, fine-grained canvas texture should be visible across the surface, paired with a matte finish. Avoid an overly clean, glossy, or plastic look; the art should feel tangible and painterly.

### Brushwork
Brushwork should be confident and visible, but used strategically. Use sharper, more defined strokes and blended rendering for focal points like faces, armor, and key objects. In backgrounds, skies, and atmospheric effects (smoke, magic), use looser, more expressive, and even impasto-like strokes.

### Light
Lighting is the most critical element. It must be dramatic, directional, and high-contrast, creating a strong chiaroscuro effect with deep, rich shadows and a bright key light. This key light often originates from a magical element within the scene, casting colored light. Always use prominent rim lighting to define silhouettes and separate subjects from their backgrounds.

### Colour
Palettes are deliberate and controlled. The primary approach is a desaturated, earthy base (stone grays, deep greens, murky browns) that is punctuated by a single, vibrant, high-saturation accent color, typically from the main light source. For more hopeful or awe-inspiring scenes, a warm, harmonious 'golden hour' palette is also on-model.

### Detail & texture
Detail is a tool for guiding the viewer's eye. Concentrate high-fidelity detail and convincing textures (worn metal, rough-hewn stone, woven fabric) in the focal area of the painting. Towards the periphery, detail and texture should become looser, more simplified, and suggestive.

### Figures
Render figures and creatures with a solid, volumetric feel rooted in believable anatomy and weight. Use the dramatic play of light and shadow to model their forms, not hard linework. Faces should be handled with a blend of realism and subtle idealization.

### Backgrounds & atmosphere
Create a powerful sense of depth and scale using atmospheric perspective. Distant elements must recede by becoming progressively lighter, less saturated, and softer in focus. Haze, fog, and murky depths are excellent tools for pushing the background back and keeping focus on the foreground.

### Mood
Strive for a mood that is consistently epic, dramatic, and fantastical. Depending on the scene's lighting and color, this can range from solemn, grim, and mysterious to awe-inspiring and heroic.

### Keep it on-model
- Is the lighting dramatic and high-contrast (chiaroscuro)?
- Is there a clear focal point defined by a strong value structure?
- Does it have a painterly feel with a matte, textured finish?
- Is detail concentrated in the focal area, with looser work elsewhere?
- Is there a strong sense of depth from atmospheric perspective?

## Watch for

- The painting looks too clean, polished, or digital, lacking the intended painterly feel and subtle canvas texture.
- The lighting is too flat or ambient, missing the strong directional source, deep shadows, and high contrast needed for drama.
- Detail is too uniform across the image, which flattens the composition instead of guiding the eye to a sharp focal point.
- The color palette is chaotic or uniformly saturated, failing to use a muted base to make a key accent color pop.
