# UX Checklist

Review each screenshot and answer YES or NO. Any NO means the gate fails.

1. Are all five lanes on each row visible and unobstructed?
2. Does every relic show artwork with a readable name?
3. Is all text fully visible and non-overlapping?
4. Does the hand sit below the field rather than on it?
5. Is the enemy hand count readable?
6. Does every card slot show a proper card face?
7. Are the two attunement meters readable as a number?
8. Is the End Turn button fully visible?
9. Does the right HUD column have clear space to its left?
10. Does the description plaque sit entirely in the left gutter when shown?
11. Is every UI element fully inside the screen viewport?
12. Would a new player know what to tap next?
