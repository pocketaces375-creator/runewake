## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Runewake ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you play Runewake: The Buried Age (the "Game"). We are committed to your privacy. The Game is designed to be **offline-first** — it works without an internet connection, and no data is collected unless you opt into cloud sync features.

By downloading and playing the Game, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 2. What Data We Collect

When you enable cloud sync (optional), the Game collects the following minimal data:

- **Device ID** — A random, non-identifying UUID generated on your device. Used to associate your save data with a server-side account so relics can sync across your devices. This is **not** a hardware identifier, IMEI, or serial number.
- **Anonymous Telemetry Events** — Basic gameplay events such as: duel started, duel ended (win/loss), node cleared on the world map, relic minted, and tutorial step reached. These events contain **no personal data** — only event type names and timestamps.

No account registration, email address, name, phone number, or precise location is required to play.

---

## 3. What We Do NOT Collect

The Game **does not** collect:

- Your name, email address, or phone number
- Precise geolocation
- Payment information (all purchases are handled by Apple App Store / Google Play directly)
- Contacts, photos, or other device data
- Browsing history or behavioral advertising data
- Biometric or health data

---

## 4. How Data Is Used

The data we collect is used only for:

- **Relic ledger sync**: Your device ID lets us store your minted Lost Relic instances on our server so they can be restored on another device.
- **Game balance improvement**: Aggregated, anonymized telemetry helps us understand which encounters are too hard or too easy, and whether the tutorial is effective.

We do **not** use your data for advertising, profiling, or selling to third parties.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

The Game uses **Supabase** (supabase.com) as its database and backend provider. Supabase stores:

- Account records (device ID → account UUID)
- Relic instance records (minted relic metadata)
- Telemetry events (anonymous gameplay data)

Supabase's privacy policy is available at: https://supabase.com/privacy

The Game contains **no** third-party analytics SDKs, ad networks, or tracking libraries. The Game does not use Google Analytics, Firebase, Unity Ads, or any similar service.
