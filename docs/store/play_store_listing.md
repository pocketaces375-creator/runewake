## Full Description (4000 chars plain text)

You are a Delver in a world that forgot itself.

Runewake: The Buried Age is a single-player digital TCG that blends strategic card duels with a deep archaeology metagame. You duel the Wardens of each region using runes you unearth, and every relic you pull out of the ground is permanently engraved with your name — a unique, minted artifact that no one else in the world will ever own.

A Duel System Built on Strategy

Master five Strata of magic — Verdant growth, Ember flame, Tide depth, Hollow shadow, and Dawn radiance — each with its own card pool and playstyle. Summon creatures into 5 lanes, attack across the board, use Keywords like Guard, Pierce, Swift, and Ward to outmanoeuvre your opponent, and excavate buried relics from your own deck to turn the tide. Every duel is a pure, deterministic state machine: no randomness, no scripting, just your decisions.

Explore the Node-Based World Map

The Fallow Reach is a hand-crafted region of 12 map nodes connected by an unlock graph. Choose your path through Duel nodes, Elite encounters, Shrines, Merchants, and Dig Sites. Each node you clear unlocks new paths, new challenges, and new rewards. The map is yours to explore at your own pace.

Dig for the Past

Earn Dig Charges by winning duels, then spend them at excavation sites — a grid-based minigame where you strike tiles to reveal rune fragments, shards, and Lost Relics. The deeper you dig, the greater the risk of collapse. The archaeology layer is the game's reward schedule: every duel is a shovel-stroke, every boss is a dig site, and your collection screen is a museum of things you found rather than things you bought.

Customise Your Rune Page

Unearth rune fragments and forge them into permanent upgrades. Build a Rune Page of 9/9/9/3 slots (three sets of three plus a keystone) that modifies your deck before each duel. Choose your runes to counter the Warden's theme — or double down on your own strategy.

Mint Lost Relics

Defeat a Warden Boss for the first time and the game mints a unique Lost Relic — a playable card engraved with your name, the date, and the site where you found it. These relics are genuine one-of-a-kind collectibles, tracked in a local ledger and optionally synced to the cloud. They are not NFTs. They are not sold. They are earned.

Three Regions in v1

- The Fallow Reach (VERDANT / DAWN) — 12 nodes, 6 wielders, 1 Warden, 1 Warden Boss. Free.
- Cinderhold (EMBER) — available via IAP.
- The Drowned Archive (TIDE / HOLLOW) — available via IAP.

What You Earn vs. What You Buy

The entire first region is free, with no energy timers, no gacha, and no pay-to-win runes. The only purchase is the optional expansion to Regions 2 and 3 — a content unlock, not a power unlock. Runes are earned. Cards are earned. Relics are earned. Your skill is the only thing that matters.

Fair from day one. Deep enough to last.
