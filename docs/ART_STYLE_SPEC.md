# RUNEWAKE ART STYLE SPEC v2.0 — "Storybook Brushwork"
Decided by Trikzos 2026-08-14. Do not relitigate. All card art batches are measured against this.
ANCHORS: PRIMARY Bloomweaver (vrd_r_bloomweaver) — visible painterly brushwork, lush storybook quality, Van Gogh-adjacent stroke energy, classical-Renaissance composition and light, figure integrated into a living environment. SECONDARY Thornbark Defender (vrd_c_thornbark_defender) — thick impasto texture, heavy brushwork, earthy warmth, figure fully embedded in the environment. TERTIARY Verdant Sproutling (vrd_c_verdant_sproutling) — quieter register, painted forest depth, soft light falloff. Every prompt must reference BOTH Bloomweaver and Thornbark Defender by name as texture/brushwork touchstones.
RULES: (1) Storybook brushwork is the house style — a running theme, not a straitjacket; cards may push darker (Hollow) or hotter (Ember) but hand-painted quality must read on every card. Nothing flat, nothing airbrushed-digital, nothing photoreal. (2) High fantasy forefront, always. NO sci-fi, ever. (3) Preserve: visible stroke texture, painted light (in paint, not lens effects), composed staging, authored-feeling environments. (4) Per-element palettes stay (Verdant greens/golds, Ember charcoal/flame, Tide deeps, Hollow bone/shadow, Dawn warm light) — palette shifts, brushwork doesn't. (5) NO text of any kind rendered into the art — no card names, no flavor text, no titles, no lettering, no watermarks. Names and flavor are client UI only, uniform fonts for all cards; any special rarity treatment is a deliberate client-side decision later, never left to the generator. (6) Texture is non-negotiable — hot/bright subjects (fire, gold, light) must still read as painted, not rendered. Thick impasto and visible canvas texture prevent the digital-smooth look that plagues AI art. (7) NEVER put the bare stratum name (EMBER/VERDANT/TIDE/HOLLOW/DAWN) in an image prompt — the model paints the literal word into the art (seen in every Ember FLUX batch). Describe colors and mood in plain language instead.
THE GATE (every batch, no exceptions): 6-card sample first → Telegram to Trikzos numbered → per-card veto/approve → only then the full batch runs, using approved samples as style references. The old 38-piece Ember/gemini batch is RETIRED — Trikzos reviewed and did not approve it; never ship or reference it.
GENERATOR: FLUX.2 Pro (black-forest-labs/flux.2-pro via OpenRouter) is the confirmed generator for all card art — Trikzos ruled 2026-08-16. Do not relitigate.
PROMPT SPINE: "oil painting in the style of classical storybook illustration, visible brushwork, single focal subject composed with breathing room, atmospheric depth with softly rendered distant background, warm light against cool shadow, restrained palette with selective vivid accents, painted by hand, unsigned artwork" + element colour description + card subject. Do NOT use the bare stratum codename as a colour/shorthand — describe the palette in natural language (e.g. for Ember: "charcoal greys and cool slate shadows, lit by molten orange and gold flame accents"). For each stratum, use these description anchors:
- EMBER → "charcoal greys and cool slate shadows, lit by molten orange and gold flame accents"
- VERDANT → "deep forest greens and earthy moss browns with golden highlights"
- TIDE → "abyssal blue-teal depths with pale foam edges and scattered sea-green light"
- HOLLOW → "bone-white and murky violet with patches of sickly green, shadow-heavy"
- DAWN → "warm cream and pale gold with soft amber light, dawn-sky tones"
PROMPT TONE: Restrained, authored, composed. The image should read as an illustration in a storybook — not an album cover, not a splash screen, not a cinematic still. Breathing room around the subject, atmospheric depth, selective focus.
Negatives: "digital airbrush, photorealism, 3D render, sci-fi elements, lens flare, text, typography, lettering, title text, watermark, logo, writing, captions, words, artist signature, painted signature, autograph, smooth digital rendering, glossy CGI, game splash art, airbrushed gradient, monochrome orange, oversaturated, cluttered composition, debris field, excessive sparks, heavy metal album art, subject filling entire frame, any letters or words".
