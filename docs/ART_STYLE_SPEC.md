# RUNEWAKE ART STYLE SPEC v1.0 — "Storybook Brushwork"
Decided by Trikzos 2026-08-14. Do not relitigate. All card art batches are measured against this.
ANCHORS: PRIMARY Bloomweaver (vrd_r_bloomweaver) — visible painterly brushwork, lush storybook quality, Van Gogh-adjacent stroke energy, classical-Renaissance composition and light, figure integrated into a living environment. SECONDARY Verdant Sproutling (vrd_c_verdant_sproutling) — quieter register, painted forest depth, soft light falloff.
RULES: (1) Storybook brushwork is the house style — a running theme, not a straitjacket; cards may push darker (Hollow) or hotter (Ember) but hand-painted quality must read on every card. Nothing flat, nothing airbrushed-digital, nothing photoreal. (2) High fantasy forefront, always. NO sci-fi, ever. (3) Preserve: visible stroke texture, painted light (in paint, not lens effects), composed staging, authored-feeling environments. (4) Per-element palettes stay (Verdant greens/golds, Ember charcoal/flame, Tide deeps, Hollow bone/shadow, Dawn warm light) — palette shifts, brushwork doesn't. (5) NO text of any kind rendered into the art — no card names, no flavor text, no titles, no lettering, no watermarks. Names and flavor are client UI only, uniform fonts for all cards; any special rarity treatment is a deliberate client-side decision later, never left to the generator.
THE GATE (every batch, no exceptions): 6-card sample first → Telegram to Trikzos numbered → per-card veto/approve → only then the full batch runs, using approved samples as style references. The old 38-piece Ember/gemini batch is RETIRED — Trikzos reviewed and did not approve it; never ship or reference it.
GENERATOR: FLUX.2 Pro (black-forest-labs/flux.2-pro via OpenRouter) is the confirmed generator for all card art — Trikzos ruled 2026-08-16. Do not relitigate.
PROMPT SPINE: "oil painting, visible brushwork, storybook illustration, classical composition, painted light" + element palette + card subject. Negatives: "digital airbrush, photorealism, 3D render, sci-fi elements, lens flare, text, typography, lettering, title text, watermark, logo, writing, captions, words, artist signature, painted signature, autograph".
