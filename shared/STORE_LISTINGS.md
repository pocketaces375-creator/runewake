### Full Description
"Runewake: The Buried Age is a fantasy trading card game where you are a Delver
in a world that forgot itself. Duel the wardens of each region using runes you
unearth, and every relic you pull out of the ground is permanently engraved with
your name.

FEATURES:
• Strategic five-lane combat with unique Attunement resource system
• Five distinct Strata factions (DAWN, EMBER, HOLLOW, TIDE, VERDANT)
• Node-based world map with branching paths and secret encounters
• Rune system: customize your playstyle before each duel
• Archaeology layer: dig for fragments, forge runes, mint relics
• 65+ hand-crafted cards with AI-generated art
• Fully offline single-player campaign — no internet required
• Free to play. No loot boxes. No gacha."
